# Code of conduct

We want a focused, respectful, and safe project community.

Contributors must:

- Treat people with respect across differences in experience and identity.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, sexualised attention, and discriminatory language.
- Protect private information and confidential project material.
- Accept reasonable moderator direction.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled with discretion and shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
