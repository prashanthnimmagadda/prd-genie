- `hero.webp` and `hero@2x.webp` are a capture of the signed v0.1.0-rc.4 app (2240 x 1400, light mode) showing a real model proposal on synthetic research notes. Replace them when the main window changes.
- Confirm the v0.1.0-rc.4 release page linked from the download buttons exists.
- Confirm ChatGPT Sites adds no analytics, cookies or scripts. If it does, change the footer line to "This page has no analytics, forms or tracking of its own."
- Check the page contains no em dashes.
