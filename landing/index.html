Meeting scheduler / RequirementsRevision 3

Make room conflicts visible before confirmation

Problem

Coordinators compare calendars and rooms by hand. A conflicting room can invalidate a proposed meeting late in the process.

Requirement

  • Recheck room availability before confirming the proposal.
  • When a conflict appears, block confirmation and explain the next step.
