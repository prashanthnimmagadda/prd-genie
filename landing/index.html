Open-source macOS app for writing requirements

Turn rough thinking into a PRD your team can review.

For product managers, founders and anyone writing requirements for internal teams or clients. PRD Genie turns instructions, notes and source material into traceable, review-ready PRDs. It runs on your Mac, with AI only if you want it.

What you get

A PRD that shows where every statement came from.

Statements link back to the source they came from. When a statement can’t be backed up, it’s flagged for you to check instead of being quietly trusted.

How it works

Create, review, revise. You decide what changes.

  1. 01

    Create

    Start from an instruction, your rough thinking and source material: PDF, Word, slides, spreadsheets, JPEG, Markdown or plain text. Use the default 13-section structure or one of your own reusable structures.

    Draft a PRD for a meeting scheduler from these notes.
    kickoff-brief.pdf interview-notes.md studio-overview.pptx
  2. 02

    Review

    Open a PRD you already have and check it against its sources. Uncertain statements are flagged “needs source review”, with the source excerpt alongside.

    Needs source review Each lead loses about two hours a week to this.
  3. 03

    Revise

    Ask for changes in conversation. Every AI change arrives as a proposal you accept or reject, with undo and full history.

    Each lead loses about two hours a week.

Honest about what it couldn’t read
If an image or table in a source wasn’t read, PRD Genie tells you, so you know what the draft is missing.
Export when it’s ready
Markdown, Word or PDF for sharing, plus a portable project archive for keeping everything together.

Local-first and private

Your work stays on your Mac. AI is optional.

PRD Genie is a single-user app with no account and no signup. Editing, saving, history and export all work offline, without AI.

Nothing is sent until you say so.

Nothing leaves your Mac until you choose a provider and send an instruction. Before it does, the app shows exactly what will be sent and where.

Choose how AI is used, if at all

  • Local modelRun a model on your own Mac through Ollama.
  • API providerOpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible service.
  • Sign in with ChatGPTOptional. Uses your own ChatGPT plan.
  • Manual handoffTake the request to any chat tool yourself.
  • No AIWrite, edit, save and export entirely by hand.
